## About Own Your Game

Own Your Game is a youth-led campaign supported by UNICEF that aims to amplify young people's voices on food environments, sport and healthy futures.

This website shares stories, videos, photographs and other content created by young people from around the world who have chosen to participate in the campaign.

## Information About Contributors

The names, photographs, videos, stories and other content featured on this website have been shared through UNICEF country offices and partner organisations for the purposes of the Own Your Game campaign.

Content featured on this website may be reviewed or removed where appropriate upon request.

## Information About Website Visitors

You can browse this website without creating an account or submitting personal information.

The website does not contain any registration forms, login functions or public content submission forms.

Like most websites, our hosting providers may process limited technical information, such as IP addresses, browser type and device information, in order to deliver the website securely and reliably. This information is used only for the operation and security of the website and is not used to identify individual visitors.

The website does not use visitor information for advertising purposes.

## Cookies

This website does not use cookies.

No cookies are placed on your device for advertising, analytics, tracking or personalisation purposes.

## Children and Young People

Taking care of children's and young people's information is important to UNICEF.

Own Your Game is a youth-focused campaign and this website features stories, photographs, videos and other content shared by young people from around the world through UNICEF country offices and partner organisations for the purposes of the campaign.

UNICEF applies specific protections when children's and young people's information is involved. For further information, please refer to UNICEF's Privacy Policy.

If a contributor, parent or guardian has any concerns about content featured on this website, or would like content to be reviewed or removed, please contact us using the details below.
